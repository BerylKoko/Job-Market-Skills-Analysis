01 · ROLE MIX

The dataset is not evenly distributed across adjacent roles.

Business Analyst and Product Management account for most of the matched postings. Product Analyst is too small to support strong comparison, so the analysis keeps its sample-size limitation visible instead of pretending every category is equally stable.

03 · COMPENSATION

Salary looks different once seniority enters the room.

Product Management has the highest observed median normalized salary, but that group also contains a much more senior experience mix. The analysis treats this as an observed association, not a claim that the title itself causes higher pay.

Important limitation

salary is missing for many postings, so sample size travels with every number.

BOTTOM LINE

Adjacent titles share tools. They do not share the same market.

The most useful finding is not a single salary or skill ranking. It is that role family changes the seniority mix, the technical vocabulary, and how compensation should be interpreted.
